## Overview

This course is designed to integrate the principles of data science with the practices of chemical engineering, equipping students with tools and skills essential for solving modern engineering challenges. Over the semester, students will explore foundational topics such as Python programming and data handling, progressing to advanced applications like machine learning, generative AI, and collaborative tools. The course is structured to emphasize the importance of each skill in real-world chemical engineering contexts, fostering a deep understanding of how to leverage data-driven approaches to innovate and optimize processes.

By combining theoretical concepts with hands-on activities, students will learn not only how to process and analyze data but also how to apply these insights to tackle complex engineering problems. Each section builds upon the previous one, culminating in collaborative projects and ethical considerations that mirror real-world scenarios. This approach ensures students develop both technical proficiency and professional responsibility.

The inclusion of state-of-the-art tools such as LangChain for generative AI, GitHub for version control, and Hugging Face for machine learning deployment prepares students to work at the forefront of chemical engineering innovation. These tools are integrated into project-based learning, where students tackle challenges like optimizing wastewater treatment or predicting material properties.

The course wraps up with a focus on ethical implications and exam preparation, reinforcing the importance of responsible engineering practices while consolidating the knowledge and skills acquired throughout the semester. By the end of the course, students will be well-prepared to apply data science techniques in diverse chemical engineering contexts, paving the way for sustainable and impactful solutions.
